7 Concrete Sealing Mistakes Calgary Homeowners Make

Published: November 17, 20266 min read

Concrete sealing is one of the best ways to protect your driveway and patio in Calgary—but only if it's done correctly. Harsh freeze-thaw cycles and de-icing salts can quickly break down unprotected surfaces.

Many homeowners pay for sealing but still see early scaling or cracking because a few key steps were missed. Below are the most common mistakes we see, and how to avoid them.

1) Sealing at the Wrong Time of Year

The best window is late summer to early fall when weather is stable. Sealing too late in fall can lead to poor curing, while sealing during wet spring conditions can trap moisture.

  • Avoid sealing if rain is expected within 24-48 hours
  • Follow product temperature ranges closely
  • Plan around overnight lows, not just daytime highs

2) Skipping Surface Preparation

Dust, salt residue, and oil spots block sealer adhesion. Always clean, dry, and repair first. Good prep is often 70% of a successful sealing job.

  • Pressure wash and remove all residue
  • Degrease oil-stained spots
  • Repair visible cracks before sealing

3) Using the Wrong Sealer

Not all sealers perform equally in Calgary. Penetrating options are often better for freeze-thaw protection than low-cost topical coatings that can wear quickly in high-traffic areas.

4) Applying Too Thick

Heavy coats can trap moisture and create uneven appearance. Follow manufacturer coverage rates and apply even coats to avoid streaking or hazing.

5) Ignoring Drainage Issues

Standing water accelerates scaling and cracking. Slope and downspout direction matter as much as the sealer itself. If water pools, fix grading first.

6) Waiting Too Long to Reseal

Set a maintenance cycle and inspect each spring. Resealing before damage appears is cheaper than repairs after deterioration begins.

7) Choosing DIY for Complex Jobs

Large driveways, severe wear, or premium finishes often need professional prep and application for long-term results. DIY can work for small areas, but complex projects are less forgiving.

Quick Maintenance Plan for Calgary Homes

  • Spring: inspect cracks, clean salt residue, schedule repairs
  • Summer: monitor surface wear and drainage
  • Early fall: seal before freeze-thaw season begins
  • Winter: use de-icer alternatives where possible

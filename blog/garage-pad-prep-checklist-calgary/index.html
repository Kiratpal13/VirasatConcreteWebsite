Garage Pad Prep Checklist Before You Pour

Published: November 17, 20265 min read

Most garage pad failures come from prep mistakes, not concrete itself. A strong base and proper drainage are essential for long-term performance in Calgary's climate.

Why Prep Matters More Than You Think

Concrete strength depends heavily on what's underneath. Even premium concrete can crack early if subgrade prep, compaction, or drainage is poor.

Prep Checklist

  • Confirm final elevations and slope away from structures
  • Excavate to design depth and remove soft spots
  • Install and compact granular base in lifts
  • Set forms square and verify dimensions
  • Place reinforcement and control joints correctly

Common Garage Pad Prep Mistakes

  • Under-compacted base that settles after the first winter
  • No slope, causing water to collect near the garage
  • Poor joint planning that leads to random cracking
  • Rushing pour timing during unstable weather

Before-Pour Quality Check

Before concrete arrives, confirm form dimensions, reinforcement position, base firmness, and access route for placing and finishing crews. A 20-minute pre-pour check can prevent expensive post-pour issues.

After-Pour Protection

Proper curing and traffic control are critical. Keep heavy loads off the slab until the recommended cure window passes, and plan sealing after full cure based on product guidance.

Fall Concrete Sealing Checklist Before Calgary Winter

Published: July 12, 20265 min read

Fall is your best chance to protect concrete before winter. The right prep and sealer application can prevent expensive spring repairs.

Why Fall Is the Best Sealing Window

In Calgary, fall usually offers cooler and more stable conditions than midsummer. Proper curing before deep cold sets in helps maximize sealer performance through freeze-thaw season.

Checklist

  • Inspect for cracks and spalling; repair first
  • Wash and fully dry surfaces before sealing
  • Apply sealer in stable temperatures and dry weather
  • Confirm downspouts drain away from slabs
  • Use winter-safe de-icing alternatives where possible

Common Winter Prep Mistakes

  • Sealing too late when overnight temperatures are too low
  • Skipping crack repairs before applying sealer
  • Allowing water pooling near driveways and garage pads
  • Using aggressive salt immediately on newer concrete

After Sealing: What to Do Next

Keep traffic off the surface for the recommended cure period. Through winter, clear snow promptly and avoid repeated use of harsh de-icers in concentrated areas.

Spring Follow-Up Plan

Do a quick spring inspection for cracks, edge wear, and drainage problems. Early repairs in spring prevent small defects from becoming expensive structural issues by the next winter.

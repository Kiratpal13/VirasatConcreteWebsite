Preparing Your Concrete for Winter: A Checklist

Published: July 12, 2026

Simple seasonal steps—cleaning, sealing, and checking drainage—can make a big difference in how your concrete weathers winter. Follow this checklist to reduce the risk of freeze-thaw damage.

  • Clean and repair before sealing
  • Ensure proper drainage away from slabs
  • Avoid heavy salt near new concrete

How Long Does a Concrete Driveway Last in Calgary?

Published: June 21, 20265 min read

A quality concrete driveway can last 25-30 years in Calgary, but local climate makes maintenance critical. Freeze-thaw cycles, salt exposure, and drainage issues are the biggest lifespan killers.

What Extends Lifespan

  • Proper base prep and compaction
  • Joint placement and crack control
  • Routine cleaning and timely sealing

What Reduces Lifespan

  • Poor drainage and standing water
  • Heavy salt use on young concrete
  • Ignoring minor cracks until winter

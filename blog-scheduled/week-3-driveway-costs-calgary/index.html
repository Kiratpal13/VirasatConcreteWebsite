Driveway Cost Breakdown for Calgary Homeowners

Published: June 21, 2026

Replacement and repair costs vary with size, design, base preparation, and finishes. This guide explains the main cost drivers so you can get accurate quotes and budget properly.

Cost Drivers

  • Site prep and excavation
  • Base materials and compaction
  • Finish type (exposed aggregate, stamped, broom)
  • Access and removal of old concrete

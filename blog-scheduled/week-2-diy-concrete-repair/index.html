DIY Concrete Repair: When to Try and When to Call

Published: June 14, 2026

Small hairline cracks and minor surface spalling can often be handled by homeowners with the right materials and care. We'll cover the safe DIY fixes and the warning signs that mean you should call a professional.

When to DIY

  • Hairline cracks under 1/8 inch
  • Superficial surface scaling

When to Call Us

  • Cracks wider than 1/4 inch
  • Large spalling or structural movement

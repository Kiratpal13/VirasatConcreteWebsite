7 Concrete Sealing Mistakes Calgary Homeowners Make

Published: June 7, 20266 min read

Concrete sealing is one of the best ways to protect your driveway and patio in Calgary—but only if it's done correctly. Harsh freeze-thaw cycles and de-icing salts can quickly break down unprotected surfaces.

1) Sealing at the Wrong Time of Year

The best window is late summer to early fall when weather is stable. Sealing too late in fall can lead to poor curing.

2) Skipping Surface Preparation

Dust, salt residue, and oil spots block sealer adhesion. Always clean, dry, and repair first.

3) Using the Wrong Sealer

Not all sealers perform equally in Calgary. Penetrating options are often better for freeze-thaw protection than cheap topical coatings.

4) Applying Too Thick

Heavy coats can trap moisture and create uneven appearance. Follow manufacturer coverage rates.

5) Ignoring Drainage Issues

Standing water accelerates scaling and cracking. Slope and downspout direction matter as much as the sealer itself.

6) Waiting Too Long to Reseal

Set a maintenance cycle and inspect each spring. Resealing before damage appears is cheaper than repairs.

7) Choosing DIY for Complex Jobs

Large driveways, severe wear, or premium finishes often need professional prep and application for long-term results.

Why Concrete Sealing Matters: A Homeowner's Guide

Published: June 7, 2026

Sealing concrete is one of the highest-ROI maintenance tasks for homeowners. A proper seal reduces water infiltration, resists de-icing salts, and slows wear from traffic and UV. In Calgary's freeze-thaw climate, sealing can prevent the small cracks and spalling that lead to expensive repairs.

Quick Tips

  • Seal in late summer or early fall when temperatures are stable.
  • Choose a breathable sealer for exterior slabs.
  • Pressure wash and repair visible damage before sealing.
